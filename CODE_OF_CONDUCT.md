# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make taking part in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behavior that makes for a positive environment: being kind and respectful, giving and accepting constructive feedback, taking responsibility for mistakes, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior: sexualized language or imagery, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, public or private harassment, publishing someone's private information without their permission, and other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer, [@cosmos-127](https://github.com/cosmos-127), is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that do not follow them, and will say why when they do.

This applies in all project spaces, and when someone represents the project in public. To report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer on GitHub. Every report is reviewed and handled fairly and in confidence.

Consequences, in order: a private warning, a public warning, a temporary ban from the project, a permanent ban.
